# <Legacy slice>: keep-or-fix decisions

Behaviour in the old slice that is odd, wrong or undocumented. Kept quirks become requirements. Fixed ones become requirements for the corrected behaviour. **The decisions belong to a person, at the gate.** The agent suggests; it never decides. IDs such as `KF-1` let a fixed requirement name its entry.
